# 🏛️ Vignan's Student Assignment Portal — Professional College Edition

An enterprise-grade, professional university assignment and coursework management dashboard for students of **Vignan's Foundation for Science, Technology & Research (Deemed to be University)**.

---

## 🏛️ Institutional Features & Professional UI

- **Institutional Authentication & SSO**:
  - Official university credentials sign-in with student ID / registration number verification.
  - One-click **Continue with Google (@vignan.ac.in)** collegiate SSO flow.
  - Student enrollment registration with department and semester tracking.
- **Academic Overview & KPIs**:
  - Live student profile banner displaying official Registration Number (`211FA04392`), department (`Computer Science & Engineering`), and academic standing (`CGPA 8.7`).
  - Institutional Coursework Completion Rate progress bar.
  - 4 Enterprise KPI Cards: **Total Coursework**, **Pending Submissions**, **Overdue Tasks (Late Alerts)**, and **Submitted & Verified Records**.
- **Coursework Management & Tracking**:
  - Real university course codes: `CS308` (Network Security), `CS304` (DBMS), `CS302` (Operating Systems), `MA201` (Engineering Math III), `ME204` (Thermodynamics), etc.
  - Formal submission forms with faculty instructor assignments, deadline presets, priority tiers (High, Medium, Low), and submission guidelines.
- **Academic Focus Session Timer**:
  - Built-in 25-minute focus session and 5-minute break timer with Start, Pause, and Reset controls.
- **Organized Academic Sections**:
  - **Timeline Schedule**: Action-required Overdue submissions, 48-hour notices, Upcoming coursework, and Completed archive.
  - **By Course Module**: Grouped under formal university course codes with completion ratios.
  - **By Priority Tier**: High (Crucial), Medium (Standard), Low (Flexible).
